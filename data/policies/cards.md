# Ledgerly Bank: Cards

> Fictional bank. All figures are invented for testing purposes.

## Lost or stolen cards

If a card is lost or stolen, the customer should freeze it immediately in the mobile app under Cards > Freeze card, or call the 24/7 helpline on 800-LEDGER.

A frozen card can be unfrozen at any time from the app. If the card is reported as stolen, it is permanently blocked and a replacement card is issued free of charge.

Customers are not liable for unauthorised transactions made after the card was reported lost or stolen.

## Card delivery

A new or replacement card is delivered to the registered address within 5 business days. Express delivery within 2 business days is available for AED 20.

## Disputed card transactions

A customer can dispute a card transaction within 60 days of the transaction date, from the app under Transactions > Report a problem.

The bank acknowledges a dispute within 2 business days and aims to resolve it within 45 days. A temporary credit may be applied while the investigation is in progress.

## Contactless payments

Contactless payments are limited to AED 500 per transaction without a PIN. Customers can switch contactless off in the app.
