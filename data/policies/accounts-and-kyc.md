# NovaBank: Accounts and KYC

> Fictional bank. All figures are invented for testing purposes.

## Opening an account

UAE residents aged 18 or over can open an Everyday Current Account or a Smart Savings Account in the mobile app in about 10 minutes.

Required documents: a valid Emirates ID and a valid passport. Non-residents cannot open an account through the app and must visit a branch.

There is no minimum opening deposit for the Everyday Current Account. The Smart Savings Account requires a minimum opening deposit of AED 1,000.

## Savings interest

The Smart Savings Account pays 3.25% annual interest, calculated on the daily balance and credited monthly. The rate is variable and may change with 30 days' notice.

## Keeping information up to date (KYC)

Customers must update their Emirates ID in the app before it expires. If the Emirates ID on file has expired for more than 30 days, outgoing transfers are blocked until it is updated.

The bank may ask customers to confirm their employment and source of funds periodically, as required by Central Bank regulations.

## Closing an account

An account can be closed from the app if its balance is zero and no cards or loans are linked. Otherwise, the customer must contact the call centre. Closing an account is free of charge.
