# NovaBank: Transfers and Limits

> Fictional bank. All figures are invented for testing purposes.

## Daily transfer limits

The default daily limit for transfers through the mobile app and online banking is AED 50,000 per customer, across all accounts.

Customers can raise the daily limit up to AED 250,000 from the app under Settings > Limits. Any increase requires one-time-password (OTP) confirmation, and the new limit becomes active after a 12-hour cooling-off period.

Transfers to a newly added beneficiary are limited to AED 10,000 during the first 24 hours after the beneficiary is added.

## Processing times

Transfers between NovaBank accounts are instant, 24/7.

Local transfers to other UAE banks made before 2:00 PM on a business day are credited the same day. Transfers made after 2:00 PM, or on weekends and public holidays, are processed on the next business day.

International SWIFT transfers usually take 1 to 3 business days, depending on the currency and the intermediary banks.

## Cancelling a transfer

Instant transfers between NovaBank accounts cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

A scheduled transfer can be cancelled from the app until 11:59 PM on the day before its execution date.

To recall an international transfer that has already been sent, the customer must contact the call centre. A recall is not guaranteed and a recall fee of AED 50 applies.

## Beneficiaries

Customers can add up to 100 beneficiaries. Adding a beneficiary requires OTP confirmation. The IBAN of a UAE beneficiary must start with "AE" and contain 23 characters.
